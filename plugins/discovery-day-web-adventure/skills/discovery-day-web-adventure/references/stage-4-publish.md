# Stage 4 - Share

## Purpose and milestone

Purpose: finalize the attendee's approved workspace-private Sites page and hand back its internal sharing link.

Milestone: the attendee explicitly authorizes sharing, the final private version is verified, and the attendee receives its workspace-only URL.

## Present the sharing summary

Use the stage label **Web Adventure - Stage 4 of 4: Share**. Before sharing, show a concise summary of the site title, intended audience, page purpose, key sections, known placeholders or limitations, and its workspace-private destination.

Resolve material ambiguities before continuing. Confirm that the site stays private to the workspace. Never make it public, share it outside the workspace, or replace another attendee's site.

## Require explicit confirmation

Ask a direct question such as:

> Shall I share this approved workspace-private site with you now?

Approval of the prototype or permission to continue is not sharing authorization. Wait for a clear yes.

## Finalize and verify

After confirmation, ensure the approved version is deployed to the attendee's existing workspace-private Sites page. Preserve the approved content and behavior. Inspect the returned deployment state and open the private URL to verify that it loads.

If finalization fails, explain the blocker and keep the existing private preview intact. Do not claim sharing succeeded until Sites returns success and the rendered private page is available.

## Hand off

Give the attendee the workspace-private URL, explain that only workspace members can access it, tell them where to make future changes, and note any known limitation. Announce Stage 4 complete, congratulate them on finishing the Web Adventure, and remind them to collect the Share stamp.
